<img src=svg/atronaut.svg align=right title="Hey ya! Welcome to the Gnosmos!" /> **Asteroids** are blogs and small websites gravitating around https://gno.land, a distributed platform utilizing **Go smart-contracts**.

Asteroids can be thought of as *gno-smart-contracts-enabled markdown wikis*.

The idea is that they orbit gno.land (using gnoweb) without necessarily putting all their content on-chain (they use simple interconnected markdown documents). Also, they allow theming. 

Asteroids are highly experimental and may become entirely realm-based in the future. They started as a proof of concept fork of gnoweb to experiment with themes, but proved usable and convenient enough to became a small project. If the project came to fruition we imagine it could be useful for bloggers, cosmos-enthusiasts, tinkerers. 

A special feature of asteroids is that by simply typing a link to something beginning by `/r`, it will run that smart-contract on gno.land and **render** it **on this very asteroid**. You may for example try to click:

* [/r/demo/art/gnoface:2025](/r/demo/art/gnoface:2025)
